# Interface design

Status: Sonnet 5.5 interface implementation and successive refinements, 2026-10-10. The coordinator verifies compilation, rendered layouts and browser interactions; see [interface QA](../reviews/interface-qa.md). The pass-one design history below precedes the later Live integration described near the end of this document.

**Current state: see "Environment, equipment and refrigerant probes" at the very end, which refines the "Spatial sandbox" section before it.** Everything before it is historical pass notes. In particular the three-column workbench, the left configuration sidebar (pinned Outdoor air, Rooms / Equipment / Pipes / Advanced tabs), the right inspector column, the 1100×860 scene, the 112 px room corner panel and the corner outdoor card with fan-outlet and ice readouts are **superseded** by the spatial sandbox. Where older sections describe a phase-coloured tube with a thin temperature core, casing dimension lines or text, the 500 mm scale bar, the Advanced "Casings and dimensions" fold, or a read-only outdoor weather card, the later refinements supersede them. The one-header, readings-popover, static-fan and vector-zone decisions of the simplification pass are unchanged and still current.

**Examples** retains explicitly illustrative fixture readings. **Live** uses the separate worker engine and source-aware readings, with generic performance assumptions and actual scientific acceptance tracked in [the engine review](../reviews/engine-science-review.md). Source switching never relabels fixtures as calculations.

## Visual choices

- **Composition.** Dark ink header, thin metric ribbon, then a three-column workbench: left sidebar (pinned outdoor air above configuration tabs), the zoned simulator workbench (centre), inspector (right), plus a collapsible diagnostics/fixtures/provenance drawer. The scene is the focus; side panels are quiet paper-coloured surfaces.
- **Palette.** Warm paper neutrals and ink for chrome; steel-blue accent. Phase colours are reserved for refrigerant: vapor purple `#7c4fba`, liquid teal `#12968e`, two-phase amber with dark diagonal stripes, idle grey. Warm/cool air tints are separate from them.
- **Temperature** is a thin core line inside each tube on a blue → neutral → red ramp (−10…105 °C), independent of phase. Segments without a fixture temperature (condenser/evaporator mid-passes) show no core line rather than an interpolated value.
- **Type.** System UI stack only; tabular numerals for readings; small-caps style labels. No remote fonts or assets.
- **Scene.** Pure SVG at viewBox 1100×860. One physical scale for both casings, 0.32 px/mm (outdoor 307×235, indoor 255×94 for 958×734 and 798×295 mm), with a 500 mm scale bar and dimension lines. There is no building drawing. Two flat zones: **OUTDOOR** (left, cool blue-grey field with a configured-weather card — glyph by configured dry-bulb, temperature and RH labelled "configured" — and a dashed base line under the outdoor unit) and **INDOOR** (right, warm neutral field). A plain grey boundary strip between them carries only the refrigerant pass-through sleeves. Each room is one rounded card, stacked top→bottom (Attic, First, Ground, then generic rooms 4–5), showing name · volume, input temperature · setpoint, the indoor unit, supply-air text and airflow, with a faint wash tinted by the room *input* temperature. The circuit geometry did not change.
- **Outdoor machine** (generic explanatory circuit, not a Daikin layout): finned coil face, 12-pass serpentine, fan, compressor with D/S badges, accumulator, four-way valve with D/S/O/I badges and both internal connection pairs drawn (active pair coloured, inactive faint), one EEV per branch on the liquid stub, common gas manifold with the physical service port, and two virtual taps with dials. Crossing tubes use a halo so gas visibly hops over the liquid header.
- **Tubing.** Each branch is a gas/liquid pair with insulation sleeve and nested risers so pairs never cross outside the casing. Tube width follows configured OD.
- **Flow.** Moving dashes (CSS animation, direction reversed by `data-dir`) plus a static arrowhead per tube. Idle: grey tubes, no dashes, no arrows.
- **Frost and defrost.** A scalable ice slab, frost veil over the fins, icicles and snowflakes grow with the preview ice mass (starts 0.10 kg, grows at the fixture's 1.57 kg/h, scale to 0.6 kg). Defrost shows reverse flow, an amber glow around the coil, dripping meltwater and a puddle, fans stopped, and melts the ice at 8.10 kg/h.
- **Owner vs generic.** Owner: light casing, "Daikin 3MXM52N8", three ports; unused ports are capped when fewer than three heads. Generic: graphite casing with dashed interior, "Generic compatible outdoor", one port per head, generic indoor shells.

## Behaviour

- Header: Heating/Cooling, example state (Normal, Frosting, Defrost, Idle; frost and defrost disabled in cooling), preview time, speed 0.5×/1×/2×/5×/10×, pause/resume, reset.
- Choosing an example **loads its conditions once** into the configuration (outdoor air, room temperatures). Later edits change configuration only. A scene banner states "Configuration ≠ fixture" and which inputs differ; the pinned block gives a one-line comparison.
- **Control hierarchy.** Permanent: header (mode, example, speed, pause, reset), ribbon banner, and the **Outdoor air** block pinned at the top of the sidebar (outdoor temperature and humidity slider + number, "configured" label, one-line fixture comparison, "Match fixture" when different). One click: tabs **Rooms** (default), **Equipment**, **Pipes**, **Advanced**. Deeper: Advanced folds and the diagnostics drawer.
- Pinned outdoor air: one slider pair, never duplicated; it stays visible on every tab and with the drawer open. Editing it changes configuration only; readings stay those of the fixture.
- Rooms (default tab): per active head volume, heat-loss coefficient, setpoint, room air temperature (inputs). Clicking or activating a room in the diagram selects it, switches to Rooms and scrolls its card into view, so inputs are one click away.
- Equipment: indoor-unit count 1–5 and per-head model first (4 or 5 switches to generic with a visible notice; owner with more than three caps the count), then owner/generic outdoor select.
- Advanced (folds): Display (bar(a)/bar(g), taps, detail labels; gauge = absolute − 1.01325), Compressor ηis, Refrigerant list (R32 selected, others "Research only", collapsed), Casings and dimensions (collapsed; coil surface and UA "—").
- Pipes: per-branch one-way length (owner-reported 14 / 6.5 / 7.5 m for attic / first / ground), liquid and gas OD, total length checks against the 2019 family-guide limits for the owner preset only.
- Selection by click or keyboard (Tab, Enter/Space, Esc): outdoor casing, compressor, reverser, accumulator, coil, fan, each EEV, service port, each branch line set, each indoor room. The inspector shows role, phase, fixture readings and configuration separately.
- View presets: Overview, Outdoor unit (3× zoom reveals detail labels), Rooms; animated viewBox unless reduced motion is requested.
- Drawer: ledger and arithmetic closure checks, fixture list (real buttons), provenance table.
- Performance: inputs are built once and refreshed without touching a focused input; subscribers diff; the scene is rebuilt only when unit count or outdoor kind changes; animation frames update only the clock text and ice transforms.
- Accessibility: focus rings, `aria-pressed` selection, `prefers-reduced-motion` stops animation (static arrowheads remain).

## Fixtures and limits

- Source: `research/model-validation-plan.md` §6: heating normal, frosting, wet cooling, reverse-cycle defrost, idle. R32 states from a pinned EOS probe; flows, fans and splits are chosen, not solved.
- Branch values for 25/35/25 reuse the fixture arrays. Any other head set takes illustrative weighted shares (0.30 / 0.40 / 0.50 by class) of the fixture totals, so sums are preserved but allocations are not physical.
- Mid-coil temperatures, EEV opening, UA, coil surface, and idle pressures are `—`. Outdoor-air coil outlet and RH details appear only in the ledger.
- Room volume, heat loss, setpoint, ηis, pipe lengths/ODs, RH and dry-bulb never alter readings. Weather glyph and ice animation are decorative previews.
- Single-cycle fixtures do not predict manufacturer performance. Defrost duration is not a controller.
- Exact N8 circuit, indoor suffixes, as-built lines, elevations and installed charge are unknown (see `research/owner-installation.md`).

## Refinement pass (changed behaviour)

- **Preview mass ledger (SC-06).** `Clock` holds `iceKg` and `retainedWaterKg`, explicitly illustrative; no drainage, refreezing or heat-transfer solver. Frosting adds ice at the fixture rate (the graphic saturates at 0.6 kg, the mass is not capped). Defrost moves `min(ice, meltRate·dt)` from ice to retained liquid. Both masses persist across idle, mode and example changes; only Reset clears them. The 0.10 kg example start is loaded only when selecting frost/defrost with no ice and no retained liquid, with a visible notice and a persistent overlay note until Reset; melted inventory is never re-seeded.
- Preview time and the fixture are frozen snapshots. After ice is exhausted in defrost, melt, drips and glow stop and the overlay and coil inspector state that readings do not change and this is not a predicted defrost duration. Drips follow actual melting (`data-melting`, false when paused or exhausted); puddle size and opacity follow `retainedWaterKg`. Ice stays drawn in idle and cooling when retained, labelled a synthetic snapshot transition.
- The overlay chip and coil inspector show current ice and retained liquid; the defrost fixture's 0.10 kg is labelled as the fixture example, distinct from the scene amount.
- **Stop valves (SC-07).** A common GAS STOP (legend p) sits on the common gas node with the physical service fitting on it; a common LIQ STOP (legend q) sits on the outdoor liquid header upstream of the branch EEVs. Both are brass round-body symbols with a stem and knob, static fully open, selectable, with inspector text. Branch EEVs keep copper bodies and carry numbered badges (full label via inspector). Placement is generic and family-informed (N2-family manual legend, printed page 31); exact N8 layout unknown. Pipe links are unchanged.
- **Outdoor zoom layout.** viewBox `4 500 346 310`; short labels (`4-way valve`, `SVC · HIGH/LOW`, `GAS STOP`, `LIQ STOP`); service gauge moved above the service fitting, virtual HP gauge below the discharge line, virtual LP gauge below the suction line; depth label right-aligned inside the stage. The legend is a reserved third stage row below the scene in every view (never floating over equipment): one slim line with phase swatches (purple vapor, teal liquid, striped amber two-phase, idle), the independent core-temperature ramp, and a `Details` button (`aria-expanded`) that reveals the caveat note inline, growing the row instead of covering the scene.
- **Pipe guide.** Owner preset checks per-branch 3–25 m and total ≤ 50 m, named family guidance (2019 N2 variant), not verified N8 installation compliance; each out-of-range branch is listed; heights unknown.
- **Chrome fixes.** Sidebar tabs size to content and fit 318 px; `.app` clips to its grid to avoid the 1 px overflow (untested here); ArrowLeft on the first tab wraps to the last; reducing the unit count clears a selection that points at a removed room, EEV or branch.

## Live / Examples integration pass (written without a build or browser run)

- **Experience.** `ui.experience` (`'live' | 'examples'`, default `'live'` after the guarded numerical and independent review gates passed) selects the single reading source. Header: Live/Examples selector, Heating/Cooling, then either the Example state control (Examples) or an automatic operating-state badge (Live: loading, fault, running, idle, reversing, defrost, draining). Live Heating/Cooling calls `store.setMode`, which edits `cfg.mode` only. Examples keep `loadExample` (fixture conditions loaded once). Choosing an example in the drawer switches to Examples.
- **One adapter.** `currentReadings(s)` in `state.ts` wraps `readingsFor`. Live values are withheld (every metric null, no cycle, no flow) while the engine is loading, errored, faulted, or has not yet applied the requested mode. `electricalKw` stays null instead of the adapter's 0. COP is suppressed in live defrost; signed room heat stays. Ribbon, scene, inspector and drawer all consume it with `branchesFor`, `readingFlowMode` and `segmentFor`.
- **Status strip.** A second ribbon row always names the basis in live (generic R32, real property backend, performance not manufacturer-calibrated), the idealized-field-line exclusion, last step CPU time, any fault, and up to three warnings inline (the rest are counted and listed in Diagnostics). Examples show the preview banner there.
- **Scene.** Pipes use `segmentFor` (closed branches idle, no animation). Indoor fans and air arrows follow each room's own fan power (`data-fan` per room). Room wash uses the engine room temperature; the per-room corner panel (see "Corner readout panels") shows actual temperature and RH, the setpoint control, and the supply line with branch heat. Ice, liquid film and melting are read from the engine film state each frame; the preview ledger is not advanced or consulted in live.
- **Sidebar.** Outdoor air stays pinned and reads "drives live model" in live; "Match fixture" exists only in Examples. Rooms always offers volume, heat-loss coefficient, setpoint; the temperature slider is "Initial room temperature (on Reset)" in live with a read-only "Now" line. Equipment keeps count and outdoor model first and states that model references never change live performance. Pipes offers only the idealized field-line mode and says length and diameter are illustration-only. Advanced holds a "Generic model parameters" fold (UA in W/K, air mass flow in kg/s, deposition area in m², displacement in cm³/rev, maximum speed in rps; bounds 0.2×–5× the engine default) and a disabled "Charge, leaks, oil and other (pending)" list.
- **Inspector and drawer.** Labels follow the source: "fixture" appears only in Examples. Quality is shown only where the engine reports it (null renders as an em dash). Live diagnostics list the engine ledger, per-room humidity, metal and fabric temperature, energy and water residuals, shared-pressure and cycle closure, iterations and CPU time, with no invented pass/fail tolerance.
- **Branch refrigerant endpoints.** The live indoor-unit inspector has an "Actual branch refrigerant inlet / outlet" block showing `engine.cycle.branchInlets[i]` and `branchOutlets[i]`: T [°C], h [kJ/kg], phase, and mass quality only where `qualityMass` is non-null (else an em dash). Rows stay and read "—" when `currentReadings` is unusable. The common compressor suction state is never used for them. The generic cooling control/model permits unequal branch outlet enthalpy and phase before the returns mix; this is attributed to the generic model, not manufacturer firmware. In live, the separate coil gas/liquid end phase rows are dropped as duplicates; fixture mode keeps them.
- **Open items for root.** The UI relies on a store notification when a new engine output arrives (header badge, ribbon, drawer and inspector refresh through `store.emit()`); `store.resetEpoch` increments on every reset; the field-line mode is a runtime-owner flag (`PHYSICAL_FIELD_LINES_AVAILABLE`, now enabled); Physical is an advanced opt-in and the default stays Idealized (reference).

## Heat-exchanger cleanliness pass (written without a build or browser run)

- **Meaning.** Cleanliness is % clear air passage, 100 (clean) to 10 (90 % obstructed). Live: a generic clear-area surrogate scaling air-side conductance and air mass flow; refrigerant-side UA unchanged; fans draw rated constant power (no fan/system curve). Examples: configuration and illustration only, fixed readings do not change. No manufacturer lookup; 10 % is not promised to stay operating.
- **Controls.** Global slider at the top of Equipment (default 100). The outdoor coil and each room card have a "Use global cleanliness (N % clear)" checkbox; unchecked reveals a stable override slider that starts at the current effective value. Checking again stores `null`. Inputs are created once; the slider is hidden, never rebuilt.
- **Data.** `RoomConfig.cleanlinessOverridePct: number | null` (default null); `Config.live.cleanlinessPct` and `outdoorCleanlinessOverridePct` come from runtime config. Effective values come from `outdoorCleanliness` / `indoorCleanliness` in `state.ts`. The UI never scales engine outputs.
- **Scene.** Vector haze, dust bands and spots on the outdoor fins and each indoor coil bay, count and opacity proportional to blocked fraction, absent at 100 %. Drawn under tubes and the ice layers so phase colours, frost and manometers remain readable; the group `<title>` states the % and provenance (global/override).
- **Inspector.** Outdoor coil and indoor unit show effective cleanliness with global/override tag. Read-only; editing stays in the sidebar so focus survives frequent updates.
- **Pipes.** Field-line selector offers "Physical (advanced)" when `PHYSICAL_FIELD_LINES_AVAILABLE` (enabled; default Idealized). The choice and its note stay concise: slower, uncalibrated homogeneous loss approximation, assumed bore, level elevation, outdoor-air exposure, not the installed losses, some regimes may fail visibly. The detail lives in "Advanced pipe assumptions": omitted kinetic energy, slip, fittings, oil and line charge (bounded approximation); the cooling controller targets each returning branch at common suction superheat after line heat, separate from the idealized conductance-weighted branch allocation and mixed-return control; unsupported unequal-coil transients or high-loss regimes fail visibly with conserved inventory. No dialog or permission flow. "Advanced pipe assumptions" holds wall (0.8–1.5 mm configured floor) and insulation conductance (0–1 W/(m·K)); the readout lists effective wall and bore per line using `pipeWallMmForOd`, labelled declared family minimum, not measured.
- **Bounds.** Generic sliders stay 0.2×–5× default except maximum compressor speed, whose lower bound is the engine's `speedMinRps`.

## Corner readout panels (historical: superseded by the spatial sandbox)

Owner steering: clearly visible numeric values in the corner of the room and outdoor boxes, with the requested setpoint next to the air temperature and a slider. Small temperature charts are deferred.

- **Mechanism.** Pure SVG/CSS equipment; each panel is native HTML in a `foreignObject` (`.panel-fo`, click-through). Only `.rp-ctl` elements take pointer events, so clicking a readout still selects the room underneath. The panel is a sibling of the selectable room group, never nested inside the `role="button"` group. Built once per rebuild and refreshed with diffed `setText`; the old name, sub and supply SVG texts are removed.
- **Room panel** (right of each room card, x 816–1084, 112 high, same in 1–5 rooms): name and volume; **Air** (live `currentReadings.engine.rooms[i].temperatureC` and RH, `—` while loading, faulted or idle without engine data) beside **Setpoint** (number field plus a range under it, 16–30 °C step 0.5 as in the Rooms sidebar, labelled "Setpoint for <room>"); **Supply** temperature from `branchesFor`, `— (fan off)` for a stopped fan, and branch kW (heating output and cooling removal positive, defrost labelled "signed heat" and negative when extracted). COP is not shown. In Examples, Air is the configured example input tagged "EXAMPLE input", supply and kW carry an EXAMPLE tag, and the setpoint stays the requested input. The initial room temperature stays in the sidebar.
- **Setpoint control.** Writes `cfg.rooms[i].setpointC` through `store.update`, the same setting as the sidebar; it does not reset room state or load example conditions. The number field commits valid in-range values while typing and clamps on change; a focused number field is never rewritten by sync. Controls stop click, pointerdown and key events from reaching scene selection and window shortcuts.
- **Outdoor panel** (inside the top-left weather card, right of the glyph): Ambient °C and Humidity % RH (configured inputs, shown while the engine loads), Fan air out (`outdoorOutletC`, `—` when off, faulted or loading, EXAMPLE tagged in Examples) and Coil ice kg (live film state or example clock, source named; updated per frame). The common outdoor sliders stay pinned in the sidebar.
- **Held ice inventory.** When Live is not usable (pending, applying mode, rejected input), the scene tick and the overlay chip read `simulation.inventory` (last accepted outdoor film state and its simulated time). Frost, icicles and puddle stay drawn at that mass (ice group slightly dimmed, `data-ice="held"`); the corner readout shows the kg with "held · t m:ss" in the warn colour, and the chip reads "Held Outdoor ice … kg · liquid film … kg" with a dashed border and a note naming the current input state. Melting, drips and glow animate only from usable engine output with `meltPowerW > 0`. With no inventory (start/reset) the value is `—` and no ice is drawn. Pressures, COP, fan outlets and room sensor values remain blank; Examples use only their own clock and never the inventory.
- **Limits.** Panels sit in the right of the room card and the weather card (x ≤ 338) so they avoid hardware, paths, dirt, frost and manometers. The Outdoor-unit zoom view starts at y 500 and does not include the weather card, so ambient values are visible in Overview and Rooms only.

- **Indoor-unit On/Off.** A native `role="switch"` checkbox in each room's scene card header and a matching switch at the top of each sidebar Rooms card both write `cfg.rooms[i].enabled`; targets, volume, loss and cleanliness stay editable while off. - **Dimensions removed.** No casing rulers, dimension text, 500 mm scale bar, inspector H×W×D rows, Advanced dimensions fold or provenance dimension rows. `OUTDOOR_CASE_MM`, `INDOOR_CASE_MM`, `MM_PX` and the drawn proportions remain; provenance keeps a dimension-free "Casing proportions" row. Refrigerant OD and room volume stay.
- **Outdoor corner card.** Enlarged to 322 × 194 (x 16–338, y 38–232): weather glyph column, then, per control, a label row (label left, note right-aligned and ellipsised) above a value row (number + unit kept on one line, `white-space: nowrap`) above the slider, so labels and values never share a row. Same bounds as the pinned sidebar; both write `cfg.outdoorC` / `cfg.outdoorRh` through `store.update`, mirror both ways, and sync skips a focused input. - **Unknowns for root's render check.** Mark legibility on the narrowest coil tubes (2.6 px) and at cream 35 °C; actual `kind: 'updating'` label and detail text from `state.ts`; the Outdoor-unit zoom (starts y 500) still excludes the outdoor card; card fit at 1440×900 and 1600×1000; Examples mismatch note height.

## Simplification pass (header, Readings popover and fans still current; sidebar and inspector-column notes historical)

Supersedes the dark header, the separate ribbon row, the five speed buttons, the Live/Examples buttons and the two-row scene notices described above. Owner objection: "This looks like a CAD software." Files: `chrome.ts`, `style.css`, `scene.ts`, and `main.ts` (DOM composition only).

- **One header** (`header.topbar`, `--h-top` 72 px, light `--panel`, no second or third bar). Left to right: brand mark + title; `[aria-label=Mode]` Heating/Cooling segmented, `.hd-pause` Pause/Resume, `select[aria-label="Requested speed"]` (0.5/1/2/5/10×), `output.time`; `select[aria-label=Source]` (Live/Examples) and, in Examples only, `select[aria-label="Example scenario"]` (Normal/Frosting/Defrost/Idle; frost/defrost options disabled in cooling); spacer; `.hd-metrics` (`.hm` ×3: COP, room heat, electrical input; fixed 96/124/124 px widths, labels from `copLabel`/`roomKwLabel` so heating/cooling/defrost sign and wording stay correct); `.hd-status` (`output.opstate` badge, live only, plus `.hd-note` one ellipsised line; Examples show `.hd-ex` instead); `button.hd-readings` ("Readings", with ⚠ n when warnings exist); `button.hd-reset` (`aria-label="Reset simulation"`). No label sits above any control group; accessible names are `aria-label`s.
- **Readings popover.** `.ribbon` (still the reading component, `id=readings-pop`) is now an absolutely positioned panel under the header, hidden until `.hd-readings` is clicked (`aria-expanded`); outside click or Escape closes it (Escape returns focus to the button and does not reach the selection-clearing handler). It keeps all nine old cells (outdoor air, room air, room heat, electrical input, COP, low/high pressure, indoor supply air, outdoor outlet) and the full `.rb-status` content: live basis, field-line assumption, ms/step, fault, last-calculated-step text and every warning (no longer truncated to three), or the preview banner in Examples. The ribbon is synced while closed, so its DOM is current when opened.

|---|---|---|---|

Examples: indoor notes read `illustrative` instead of a heat value (a fixture has no per-branch coil heat); the outdoor note derives heat from the fixture `qEvapKw` (heating, negative) or `qCondKw` (cooling/defrost, positive) and adds an `illustrative` line.
