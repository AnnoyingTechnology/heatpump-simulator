## Coverage and open gaps

This batch widens the reviewed evidence beyond the published runtime baseline of 36 Daikin outdoor and 79 indoor records. It closes the current Stylish C/D finish pairing references and confirms the current Perfera class line while preserving the difference between a current family class and an archived full SKU. It also identifies all nine current residential MXM outdoor size classes/full references and maximum head counts, but it does **not** make multisplits selectable: the present runtime contract is single-port and exact per-size compatibility/capacity matrices remain unresolved.

The 2026 catalogue manifest still needs exact, source-aligned coverage for the remaining current wall families and variants (Ururu Sarara, Emura, Comfora, Sensira FTXF/FTXC and their Siesta equivalents), floor-standing and concealed ducted families, CTXA15 multi-only variants, and current/historic full-reference status. MXF Sensira multisplits and Multi+ models are not in this MXM batch. Historical references beyond the already staged 2022 family rows need a separate dated ledger. VRV/VRF, hydronic and hybrid equipment remain outside the residential air-to-air scope.
