# Bounded legacy N-generation integration

Nine exact outdoor references researched by the designated Luna agent are independently reviewed and promoted in this isolated checkout: `2MXM40N2V1B`, `2MXM50N2V1B`, `2MXM68N2V1B`, `3MXM40N2V1B8`, `3MXM52N2V1B8`, `3MXM68N2V1B`, `4MXM68N2V1B9`, `4MXM80N2V1B9`, `5MXM90N2V1B9`. They are marked legacy in the native outdoor select. The owner preset stays `owner`; its `3MXM52N8` family declaration is not silently aliased to the unconfirmed full suffix `3MXM52N2V1B8`.

All nine source columns explicitly state R-32. Their casing and published maximum connected head counts are applied as metadata. Existing room identities, indoor references, line settings, history and manual cycle/charge controls are retained. An excess head count rejects selection until rooms are removed explicitly. Missing connected minimum, per-port class/reducer constraints, class-sum ceiling and exact full-SKU eligibility are **null/unknown**. The minimum is not borrowed from A8/A9, so a one-head arrangement remains unknown rather than certified. Off heads remain physically connected. A foreign-brand reference alone stays unknown rather than proving an invalid combination.

The source tables publish cooling −10…46 °C DB for 2MXM68, both N8 models, standard 3MXM68 and both N9 families. **2MXM40/50 cooling cells are blank**, not merged numeric cells: both endpoints remain null. Heating −15…18 °C WB is published for standard `3MXM68N2V1B`; heating endpoints for the other accepted exact variants are unpublished in the reviewed tables. They remain null and never copy limits from peers. `OutdoorModelInfo.publishedOperatingRangesC` therefore allows nullable endpoints; consumers must test for null before comparisons. Unknown does not mean zero or unlimited OEM operation.

Standard 3MXM68 publishes 24.1 m³/min (1,446 m³/h) at **super-low** fan speed in cooling/heating. That source fact remains research-only and never populates a high-fan profile. Runtime high-fan flow stays null for all nine; the editable generic mass flow is retained. Factory charge, pipe limits, allocated combination outputs and electrical rating points remain documentary research; they do not calibrate inventory, UA, compressor displacement, efficiency, fan watts or an OEM performance map. The N8 family piping diagram remains research-only because full-SKU applicability and reducer/class mapping are unresolved.
