## MXZ physical readback and import blocker

The independent physical readback in `terra-mitsubishi-mxz-physical-readback-2026.json` checks all twelve staged columns against the rendered catalogue pages, including minimum and maximum connected heads and connection counts. All staged physical values match. PDF p.111 (printed p.110) names MSZ-HR25/35/42/50VFK2 and restricts HA compatibility to the MSZ-HR VFK2 family; it does not establish arbitrary combinations. The model-specific booklet combinations and their individual class/reference eligibility have not yet been independently transcribed. The 3 A maximum sum of connected indoor maximum currents is a separate mandatory rule, not a class-sum ceiling, and the typed shape cannot express it.

No MXZ acceptance or import is claimed. With `topology: multisplit`, `generate` unconditionally calls `multisplit_installation`, which requires Daikin-specific named port/class/reducer statements and a class-sum expression. Calling it on the first staged MXZ candidate with a bound review produces `ValueError: Multisplit named-port topology is incomplete`. The 2MXF/3MXF entries avoid this function because their acceptance records do not set `topology`; copying that approach would violate the requested topology and bypass the multisplit validator. The `legacy-unresolved` branch would falsely label current equipment legacy and cannot represent the published minimum. Resolving this requires a supported importer path for independently reviewed Mitsubishi installations; the requested scope prohibits changing the importer. The existing topology validator also attributes manufacturer and absent-rule findings to Daikin or legacy sources. MXZ-5F102VF3 and MXZ-6F120VF3 remain unresolved because their class combination tables are digital-only and unpinned.
