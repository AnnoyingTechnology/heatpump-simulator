## Historical reference coverage

The official Daikin France/EU installer catalogue ECPFR-FR22-000 (2022; SHA-256 `814243080bf6b8795adccb3f8c670a87d0b692d7c1c09ec32c306f04add187ad`) is retained as dated historical evidence. It covers older Ururu, Emura, Stylish, Perfera, Comfora, Sensira, FTXQ-A, floor and ducted ranges. Existing runtime Stylish facts came from this edition; it does not establish 2026 availability. Historical catalogue references remain separate from current claims, and no older family is declared complete in this ledger.

## Runtime and exclusions

The generated catalogue currently contains **36 Daikin outdoor and 79 Daikin indoor profiles**: the four earlier Stylish pairs plus 2026 Emura, Ururu, Comfora, Sensira FTXC-E, seven exact-reference Sensira FTXF indoor heads, four FDXM ducted indoor heads, twelve archived full-SKU Perfera wall heads, three Perfera floor heads, CW/CB Stylish Nepura, FTXTJ Nepura and FTXTM/FVXTM Nepura references. Five RXF F-series outdoor profiles are included as historical references because Daikin France marks those exact references archived; RXF60/71 exact outdoor identities and FTXF60/71 outdoor pairings remain unsupported. Perfera FTXM25/35/50 A2/A5 and FVXM25/35/50 references have exact accepted RXM-A9 pairs; other FTXM/FVXM sizes remain generic. FTXF20–50 heads have accepted historical RXF-F matches; FTXF60/71 and FDXM heads use generic outdoor until exact compatible outdoor profiles are accepted. This is a bounded selection, not the full Daikin consumer lineup. Multiport systems await a deliberate importer/runtime compatibility model. Multi+ tanks/hydronic equipment, VRV/VRF, Mitsubishi Heavy Industries and automotive parts are out of scope.
