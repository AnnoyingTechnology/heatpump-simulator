## Runtime and exclusions

The isolated reviewed catalogue after the ATXC, Perfera and exact Siesta outdoor additions contains **84 Daikin outdoor and 150 Daikin indoor profiles**, plus 25 Mitsubishi Electric outdoor and 45 indoor profiles (owner and generic scenario choices are separate). The Perfera addition contributes seven explicit published classes in each direction; all earlier accepted records remain unchanged. This count includes current source listings, archived exact references, availability-unknown Europe/France tables and separately marked historical N-generation outdoors; it is not a count of current France retail models or complete OEM combinations.

Current and legacy multiport models are supported as necessary installation constraints with explicit unknown exact indoor eligibility. A complete green OEM combination is never inferred from port size, class contribution or a shared manufacturer. Multisplit-specific charge caps are documentary and do not calibrate the generic refrigerant cycle. New Perfera classes apply published indoor volumetric fan flow and casing, with generic/manual outdoor airflow and generic cycle assumptions; full factory suffixes remain unpublished. FDXM exact indoor heads still lack accepted dedicated outdoor pair profiles. ATXM/ATXF full indoor refs and their ten newly accepted ARXM/ARXF outdoor profiles remain separate from FTXM/FTXF and RXM/RXF. A matching brand or numeric size does not certify an alternative pairing.

Counts above supersede the historical 41/119 snapshot in the original Luna acceptance receipt; that immutable provenance describes the earlier batch only. New bounded integration receipts in this directory document RXF D9, current/legacy MXM, Siesta exact heads/outdoors, ATXC EV1B and Perfera published classes. Hydronic floors/tanks and VRV/VRF remain deferred; they have not been implemented by catalogue work.
