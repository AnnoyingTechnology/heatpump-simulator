## Runtime and exclusions

The isolated reviewed catalogue after the floor/duct, FTXTP, exact FTXTA-D, 2MXF and 3MXF batches contains **91 Daikin outdoor and 185 Daikin indoor profiles**, plus 31 Mitsubishi Electric outdoor and 55 indoor profiles (owner and generic scenario choices are separate). The Perfera addition contributes seven explicit published classes in each direction; the FTXTP addition contributes two class pairs; the floor/duct batch contributes seven indoor classes with class-level RXM partners; the FTXTA-D batch contributes four exact France references; the 2MXF and 3MXF batches contribute four exact multisplit outdoor profiles. Class/exact partner identities are available to selection through each indoor profile’s `compatibleOutdoorClasses`; exact full-SKU eligibility remains unknown where the sources provide only classes or connection rules. This count includes current source listings, archived exact references, availability-unknown Europe/France tables and separately marked historical N-generation outdoors; it is not a count of current France retail models or complete OEM combinations.

Current and legacy multiport models are supported as necessary installation constraints with explicit unknown exact indoor eligibility. A complete green OEM combination is never inferred from port size, class contribution or a shared manufacturer. Multisplit-specific charge caps are documentary and do not calibrate the generic refrigerant cycle. New Perfera classes apply published indoor volumetric fan flow and casing, with generic/manual outdoor airflow and generic cycle assumptions; full factory suffixes remain unpublished. FDXM25/35/50 exact heads now have source-listed exact RXM pair profiles; FDXM60 exact pairing remains unknown. ATXM/ATXF full indoor refs and their ten newly accepted ARXM/ARXF outdoor profiles remain separate from FTXM/FTXF and RXM/RXF. A matching brand or numeric size does not certify an alternative pairing.

Counts above supersede the historical 41/119 snapshot in the original Luna acceptance receipt; that immutable provenance describes the earlier batch only. New bounded integration receipts in this directory document RXF D9, current/legacy MXM, Siesta exact heads/outdoors and ATXP exact partners, ATXC EV1B and Perfera published classes. Hydronic floors/tanks and VRV/VRF remain deferred; they have not been implemented by catalogue work.
