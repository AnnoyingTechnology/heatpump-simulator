         KEY FEATURES
     The ZSX series, featuring an "Elegant Timeless Design," is ideal for small to medium-sized living spaces.
     Key features include:
     • High seasonal efficiency performance rated up to A+++ in cooling and heating
     • Environmentally friendly with low Global Warming Potential (GWP) and high energy efficiency with refrigerant R32
     • Quiet airflow and extended reach achieved through jet technology
     • Available in three different colour choices for user preference
     • Wireless interface control included for convenient operation
     *SC-BIKN2-E cannot be used simultaneously with the Wireless LAN control system
